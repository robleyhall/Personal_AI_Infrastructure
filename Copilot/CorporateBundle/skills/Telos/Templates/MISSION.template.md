# Mission

*Template — replace with your own. One paragraph, present tense.*

I help enterprise customers realize measurable outcomes with Microsoft's
cloud, productivity, data, AI, and security platforms. I translate business
intent into architecture that is pragmatic, secure, and operable. I am
accountable for technical win — not just the design — across the full
customer lifecycle: discover, envision, design, validate, deliver, and
grow.

## What I commit to

- Meet the customer where they are, not where the reference architecture assumes they are.
- Favor reversible, small steps over grand plans.
- Make security and operability first-class, not retrofitted.
- Name trade-offs explicitly; never paper over them.
- Leave the customer's team more capable than I found it.

## What I refuse

- Closing on an architecture the customer cannot operate after I leave.
- Proposing a pattern I cannot defend on first principles.
- Confusing activity with progress on a technical win.
