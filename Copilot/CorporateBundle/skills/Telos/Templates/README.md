# Telos — CSA (Cloud Solution Architect) Templates

This directory contains opinionated scaffolding for a Cloud Solution
Architect's Telos entries. Templates are **role-shaped**, not
customer-specific or internally confidential. Populate your own files from
these starting points.

Files:
- `MISSION.template.md`      — your mission as a CSA
- `VISION.template.md`       — the future state you're working toward
- `GOALS.template.md`        — concrete outcomes per horizon
- `CAPABILITIES.template.md` — technical depth + breadth map
- `STRATEGIES.template.md`   — how you approach accounts, deals, and delivery
- `STAKEHOLDERS.template.md` — account team, partners, customer exec sponsors

On first write, your own content overrides every template word. Templates
are a starting posture, not a prescription.

**Do not commit customer names, account codes, internal-only project names,
or pre-GA product information to these files.** They live under
`~/.pai/USER/TELOS/` on your laptop, but the files still benefit from the
hygiene of staying free of confidential identifiers.
